Hi there! I'm **Abel** (also known as Chen online). I'm based in mainland China, and this is my personal blog where I write about things I care about — AI tools, keyboards, automation, and whatever else catches my eye.

## About Me

I communicate comfortably in both **Chinese and English**, and I spend a lot of time at the intersection of technology and design.

### The Keyboard Thing

I have a deep interest in **mechanical keyboards and keycap culture**. If you've heard terms like GMK, Group Buy, PBT, ABS, double-shot, or dye-sublimation — we'd probably get along well. I've spent more time than I'd like to admit researching pudding keycaps (PBT upper layer, translucent PC lower layer), keycap market trends, and manufacturing processes. It's a hobby that blends technical knowledge with aesthetic sensibility, and I love it.
